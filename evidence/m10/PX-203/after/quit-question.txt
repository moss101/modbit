1 task is running (shown in a pane window) and 1 background terminal is running (shown in a pane window). Quit anyway?

Quitting stops the local Core that runs your tasks.

Everything the Core accepted is already saved. Running tasks are suspended at their last saved step and recovered the next time you open Modbit; nothing continues while Modbit is closed. Choose whether they resume by themselves when you reopen it, or wait for you to resume them.

Background terminals keep running for about a minute and then stop with the Core.

The 2 pane windows close with Modbit; their panes are back in the dock when you open Modbit again.
