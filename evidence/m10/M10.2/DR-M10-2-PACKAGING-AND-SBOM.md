# Decision Record — M10.2 packaging tool and SBOM tooling

**Decision Record:** DR-M10-2-2026-10-02
**Status:** APPROVED by the owner in chat on 2026-10-02 (two numbered decisions below).
**Scope:** dependency decisions under `docs/36_BUILD_BUY_DEPENDENCY_AND_LICENSE_POLICY.md` and `docs/70_CI_CD_RELEASE_AND_SUPPLY_CHAIN.md` for task M10.2. Creates no subsystem and no second updater; packaging belongs to `desktop`, release tooling and supply chain to `governance`.

## Approved decisions

| # | Decision | Ruling |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Desktop packaging tool | **electron-builder**, pinned to an exact version. Targets: macOS dmg+zip, Windows NSIS+zip, Linux AppImage+tar.gz. Packaging only: it does not own the update client, the signed manifest or the feed (`apps/desktop/src/main/update/`, `tools/release/`). Its own auto-update feed format and `electron-updater` are NOT adopted: the signed-manifest updater is the only updater. |
| 2 | SBOM tooling | **CycloneDX JSON** produced by `cargo-cyclonedx` (Rust workspace), `@cyclonedx/cyclonedx-npm` (pnpm workspace) and `syft` (guest image and the packaged artifacts), listed in and signed through the update manifest by `tools/release`. **Implementation deviation, 2026-10-02:** `@cyclonedx/cyclonedx-npm` was not adopted. Run against the pnpm workspace it reports `missing` for packages pnpm does not lay out the npm way and only completes with `--ignore-npm-errors`, which would sign an SBOM of unknown completeness. pnpm 11's built-in `pnpm sbom --sbom-format cyclonedx` reads `pnpm-lock.yaml` and produces the same CycloneDX 1.5 document with no new dependency, so the npm half uses that, plus an explicit `electron` component for the runtime the packager copies in. `cargo-cyclonedx` is adopted as approved; `syft` (guest image, packaged artifacts) is still to be wired in CI. |
