=== root cause: std creates a child's pipes with pipe() and sets FD_CLOEXEC afterwards on macOS
rustc 1.97.1 library/std/src/sys/pipe/unix.rs:
  28: cvt(libc::pipe2(fds.as_mut_ptr(), libc::O_CLOEXEC))?;   (android, freebsd, linux, netbsd, openbsd, ...)
  34: cvt(libc::pipe(fds.as_mut_ptr()))?;                    (every other target, macOS included)
  38: fd0.set_cloexec()?;
  39: fd1.set_cloexec()?;
A process another thread spawns between 34 and 39 inherits the write end. Here the approver thread's
`approval list` pipe went to the main thread's `question answer --wait` CLI: `approval list` exited 0 but its
stdout never reached EOF, so the approver never resolved the approval, and the `--wait` CLI waited on that
approval for good. Standalone check (raw pipe()+fcntl in 4 threads, std spawns in 4 others): 84 and 143
leaks of 20000 pipes with no gap, 6042 with a 50 us gap.

=== fix: tests/support/bounded.rs SPAWN_LOCK, held across every spawn of the test binary
regression test no_spawn_inherits_a_pipe_another_thread_is_creating (8 threads x 400 `modbit-cli platform`
calls, 4 threads of /bin/sh probes reporting any pipe they hold above stdio):
mutation (lock removed: `let _ = &SPAWN_LOCK;`), 20 runs:  failed 20 of 20

sample failure without the lock (100 calls per thread): 6 of 192 probes inherited another call's pipe (descriptors: ["28", "13\n18", "39", "36\n37\n47", "24\n26", "35"])
