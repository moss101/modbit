Today, from the current build (PX-195 to PX-197)

Why it feels complex and rigid

1
Approval is a five-control formTwo native dropdowns, then Skip, Always and Run in three different styles. The decision is buried in the controls.
2
Internals leak into the screenIntent hashes, shell.exec, “Generation 9”, “prompt prefix reused 4 of 6”. True, but not what someone deciding needs first.
3
One size, one weightThread text, rows, labels and buttons all sit near 14px. Nothing leads the eye, so everything reads as equally important.
4
A fixed panel with five tabsThe right side is always there, with Changes, Terminal, Browser, Files and Evidence, even when most are empty.
5
Two lists for one thingProjects and Repositories sit in the same sidebar, and approvals live both on a page and in the thread.
6
Home is a formA path box, a checkbox and a “GitHub issue URL” link. No recents, no starters, nothing that needs you.
7
Controls scatterMode and model inside the box, location and run mode below it, then send, stop and a ring on the right.
